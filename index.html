Features

  • Rollback netcode

    A direct connection, with the game state checked every frame.

  • Online lobbies

    Rooms that play first-to-N sets.

  • Modern Practice

    • Custom dummy behaviour
    • Position reset
    • Real-time frame data (Soon)
    • Distance from your opponent (Soon)
    • Save states (Soon)

  • Replays

    Save full replays of your matches and watch them back with modern controls.

  • Ranked

    Rated sets with MMR, ranks and a leaderboard.
